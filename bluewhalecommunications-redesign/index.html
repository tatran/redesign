Book a free discovery call. Bring your goals, your questions, and the parts that feel a little tangled. We can discuss which kind of support makes sense for your organization.

Can we work with our existing team?

Yes. Advisory can support your internal team, execution can take content off your plate, and training can build skills within your organization. The mix depends on what you need.

Who do you work with?

Blue Whale works with nonprofits, mission-driven organizations, and growing businesses. The firm is based in British Columbia and works with organizations across North America.

Your Tide Chart is a 15-month communications and marketing roadmap for organizations that want a clear direction before considering ongoing support. Explore the resource or ask about it on your call.
