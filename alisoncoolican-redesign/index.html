My approach

Care shaped around you.

We start with your story, how you move, and what you’d like to get back to. That conversation guides the treatment and what we work on together.

Manual therapy

I draw on orthopaedic assessment and myofascial, visceral, and neurofascial techniques to tailor hands-on care to your needs.

Movement education

We can explore ways of moving that feel more comfortable in the activities you do every day.

Shared understanding

I’ll explain what I’m working on and why, so you can make informed choices about your care.

Meet your therapist

Hi, I’m Alison.

I’ve been a registered massage therapist since 2005. My work draws on a background in anatomy and cell biology, ongoing study of fascia, and a lifelong interest in how people move.

I’ve taught in massage therapy programs since 2008 and spoken about fascia and anatomy at the Pedorthic Association of Canada Annual Conference. Outside the clinic, I’m often biking, climbing, skiing, or practising yoga.
