COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear Answers Before You Call.

Have a specific pipe size, access question, or commercial tender? Reach out directly by phone or email.

What details are helpful when I call for an inspection?+

Your property address, the symptoms you have noticed (slow drains, backups, odours), approximate age of the building, and whether you know where the cleanout access is located.

What is CIPP (Cured-in-Place Pipe) repair?+

CIPP is a trenchless rehabilitation method where a resin-saturated liner or sectional patch is cured inside your existing pipe. Where pipe condition allows, it restores the line while protecting gardens, trees, and driveways above.

What pipe sizes can your cameras inspect?+

Our Vivax push camera inspects smaller residential and lateral lines from 50–150 mm (2"–6") up to 120 m, while our Rovver robotic crawler inspects larger commercial and municipal pipes from 150–900 mm (6"–36") up to 200 m.

Do you work with commercial, strata, and municipal clients?+

Yes. Down Under Pipe Inspection serves residential homeowners, strata properties, civil contractors, and municipalities across Vernon and the Okanagan.
