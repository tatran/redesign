Lessons

What would you like to learn?

Tell Angela what you enjoy and what you want to work on. She’ll tailor your lessons to your goals, from playing your first piece to preparing for a performance.

Piano

Build technique, read music and explore the styles you love.

Voice

Develop a healthy singing technique and find repertoire that suits your voice.

Music theory

Make sense of rhythm, notation and harmony and put them to use in the music you play.

Performance coaching

Prepare for a recital, audition or speech and feel more at ease in front of an audience.

Piano accompaniment

Work with a collaborative pianist for rehearsals and performances, as a singer or an instrumentalist.

Your teacher

Angela Sommer

Angela is a BC Registered Music Teacher who has taught for more than 25 years. She draws on different styles and teaching methods to help each student develop technique, musicianship and confidence.

She teaches from her Vernon studio and works with students across the Okanagan and Shuswap. Online lessons are available from anywhere.

Getting started

Before your first lesson

Do I need previous experience?

Beginners are welcome. Angela also works with returning students and experienced musicians looking to take the next step.

Can I take lessons online?

Yes. Ask Angela which format suits your goals. In-person lessons at the Vernon studio are by appointment.

What languages are lessons taught in?

Angela teaches in English and German.
