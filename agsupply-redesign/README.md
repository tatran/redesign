# A&G Supply homepage redesign

[English preview](https://livingpixelstudios.com/redesign/agsupply-redesign/) · [Aperçu en français](https://livingpixelstudios.com/redesign/agsupply-redesign/fr/)

This is a static homepage concept. Serve this directory with any static file server. There is no installation or build step. English is available at `/`, and French Canadian at `/fr/`. The theme follows the device until a visitor chooses light or dark mode. The browser remembers the choice across both pages.

The product catalogue, account login, business account application and service and contact forms use A&G Supply’s existing public destinations. Some of those destinations are in English.

## SEO and production

Both language pages include localized titles and descriptions, canonical and reciprocal language URLs, Open Graph metadata, a social image and LocalBusiness and WebPage structured data.

To launch on the company domain, deploy the English page and its shared assets at `https://www.agsupply.bc.ca/` and the French page at `https://www.agsupply.bc.ca/fr/`. Keep the existing catalogue, forms and resource URLs accessible. Merge the entries in `sitemap.xml` into the company’s sitemap so the existing pages stay listed. Update the canonical, alternate, social and sitemap URLs together if the final paths change.
