A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU CALL

Good questions.
Helpful answers.

Which appliance brands do you service?

We service most major home appliance brands, including Electrolux, Frigidaire, Kelvinator, White Westinghouse, Whirlpool, Maytag, KitchenAid, Amana and Kenmore. Contact us with your brand and model to confirm.

Can I buy a part without booking a repair?

Yes. We supply home appliance parts for customers doing their own repairs. Have your model and serial numbers ready so our team can help identify the right part and check availability.

What should I have ready when I contact you?

Your appliance type, brand, model and serial numbers, a brief description of the issue and your location are helpful. If you cannot find a model number, call us and we can help you locate it.

Do you offer service outside Vernon?

Yes. Our service area includes Coldstream, Lumby, Armstrong, Silver Star and Predator Ridge. Call to confirm availability for your address.
