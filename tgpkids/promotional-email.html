🔍 4 Quick Findings from Our Technical Audit of tgpkids.ca:

  • Mobile Viewport Overflow & Missing Hamburger Menu: Over 75% of parents search for Kelowna childcare on smartphones. Currently, the Wix desktop header and fixed-width containers overflow horizontally on mobile screens without a collapsible mobile menu.
  • Best Selling Points Hidden Off the Homepage: Your current homepage only shows the apple emblem, a single button, and an Instagram widget. Parents have to click through 5 separate tabs to discover that you cap classrooms at 8 children, operate two campuses (875 Hollydell Rd & 645 Matt Rd), and are led by an ECEBC Sue Fraser Award recipient.
  • Waitlist Friction & Copy Polish: The third-party Boom Form widget on /waitlist loads slowly and contains minor typos ("Please note that by completing this form does not guarantee...", "world around then", "grow in to") that can easily be polished.
  • No Interactive Program Matcher or Dark Mode: Parents cannot quickly match their child’s age (18–36m Toddler, 3–5y Preschool, or School-Age Summer Care) to a preferred Kelowna campus and immediately book a tour.
