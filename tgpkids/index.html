Frequently Asked Questions from Kelowna Families

Each classroom at TGP Kids welcomes a maximum of 8 children. This intimate, family-like environment reduces sensory overwhelm, allows personalized educator attention, and helps children of different developmental stages learn empathy and leadership together.
We care for children from 18 months up to school age (Toddler and Preschool programs), with summer out-of-school care available when possible.
We use the Brightwheel Parent Portal (accessible via the "Parent Login" button in the header). Parents receive real-time check-ins, daily activity photos, meal/nap updates, and direct messaging with our managers.
Please note that completing our inquiry/waitlist form does not guarantee immediate acceptance at The Gathering Place, as we strictly adhere to licensing ratios (max 8 children per room) to ensure optimal care. Our manager will contact you promptly with availability and tour times.
