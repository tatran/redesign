A CENTURY OF SHOWING UP

Small acts.
Lasting connections.

We’re neighbours, volunteers, and friends who believe in making our community a better place. Since 1920, Vernon Elks #45 has been part of the city’s story.

As part of the Elks of Canada, our lodge carries forward a simple idea: people coming together can make a difference. Whether you’re looking to volunteer, join the lodge, or gather your favourite people, start here.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you drop by.

How do I book the hall?+

Call 250-549-1883 and leave a detailed message with your date, guest count, and plans. The lodge will help you confirm availability and the right setup.

Can I arrange catering?+

Catering is available on request. Discuss your menu and requirements with the lodge; its published rental information asks for catering confirmation at least 14 days in advance.

How can I become a member?+

Open the membership application above, or contact the lodge for guidance on applying and getting involved.

Where can I find current events?+

Follow the event link above or contact the lodge. Activities and schedules can change, so check current details before making plans.
