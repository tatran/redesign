Before we meet

A little clarity
for the first step.

Can we meet remotely?

In-person appointments are preferred, especially for your first visit. Remote sessions via Zoom, Skype or FaceTime can be requested. Contact Phebe to discuss arrangements.

Where is the practice?

The office is at 6916 Rugg Road, Vernon, BC V1B 3S9. Published office hours are Tuesday and Thursday, 10:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., with the final appointment starting at 4 p.m. Confirm current availability when booking.

What if I’m unsure which session to choose?

You’re welcome to contact Phebe before booking. A conversation can help you understand the approaches and decide which you would like to explore.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Use the reschedule or cancel buttons in your booking confirmation email, or contact Phebe for help. The published policy requires 24 hours’ notice for a full refund; no-shows and late arrivals receive no refund or discount.

Does the French website mean sessions are in French?

This site is available in French for easier browsing. Please contact the practice to confirm the languages available for your session.
