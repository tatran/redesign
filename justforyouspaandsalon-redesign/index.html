Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit to Just For You.

We understand schedules change. We kindly request a minimum of 24 hours advance notice for single service cancellations, and 48 hours notice for group bookings, couples suites, and multi-service packages. This allows our practitioners to offer the opening to guests on our waitlist.
For weekday morning and afternoon single treatments, 3 to 7 days is usually sufficient. However, for weekend appointments, evening slots (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM), and large spa packages, we recommend scheduling 2 to 3 weeks ahead to secure your preferred day and time.
