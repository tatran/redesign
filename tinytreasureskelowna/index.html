🧠 Holistic Early Learning Architecture

Why Attachment-Based Care + 4 Proven Philosophies Matter

Recognized by local early developmental assistance programs and recommended by Kelowna Kindergarten & Grade 1 teachers, our curriculum puts emotional safety first so curiosity can flourish.

💛

1. Attachment-Based Core

Children learn best when they feel deeply safe, seen, and emotionally connected to consistent caregivers like Mrs. Tammy and Jamal.

🪵

2. Montessori Independence

Accessible hands-on trays, practical life skills, rolling dough, and self-directed discovery that build quiet confidence.

🔍

3. Reggio Emilia Wonder

Child-led science experiments, magnifying nature walks, worm & grasshopper discovery, and expressive art projects.

🍂

4. Waldorf Rhythm & Warmth

Predictable daily rhythms, storytelling, seasonal backyard play in leaves and snow, and imaginative cooperative play.
