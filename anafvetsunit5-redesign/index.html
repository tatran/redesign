Our story

The Spitfire over 46th Avenue

Unit 5 has served Vernon since 1971. Members built the full-scale Spitfire replica on the clubhouse roof using markings from the logbook of Vernon veteran and Royal Canadian Air Force pilot Philip Bodnarchuk.
