Heritage & Practice

European Pedigree & Meditative Depth

Heidi Thompson was born in Vernon, BC. At 17, she moved to Europe where she earned a Swiss Diploma in Professional Photography from the University of Art and Design in Zürich (Kunstgewerbeschule). She then apprenticed with legendary German painter Oskar Koller, studied at the Akademie der Bildenden Künste Nürnberg under Ernst Weil, and continued at the Hungarian State University for Fine Art in Budapest under Kokas Ignác.

Returning to Canada in 1982, Thompson completed her first 10-day Vipassana meditation course in 1983—a continuous daily discipline that became the foundational pulse of her art. Rather than depicting external scenery, her canvases operate as optical energy fields, dissolving boundaries between viewer, matter, and consciousness.

Critical Acclaim

There Is No Answer — The Physics of Nature

"Attuned to the artist’s role as a medium, Heidi Thompson works with the physics of nature. Neither dominating nor controlling the outcome of a composition, she deliberately flicks material with a fine fan brush, orchestrating colour, light, and surface vibration. Her work stands alongside Mark Tobey, Jackson Pollock, Mark Rothko, and Natvar Bhavsar in its soulful radiance."

John K. Grande

Distinguished Art Critic, Author of 'Balance: Art and Nature'
