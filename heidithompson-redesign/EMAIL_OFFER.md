## 1. Executive Summary & Site Teardown

Heidi Thompson's body of work represents over four decades of rigorous fine art practice, European academy training (Zürich, Nuremberg, Budapest), and continuous Vipassana meditative discipline. Her *Color Energy Fields*, *Veil Paintings*, and *Colorful Patina* canvases represent museum-grade mastery.

However, an audit of the current web infrastructure at **heidithompson.ca** reveals critical friction points:

| Current Limitation on `heidithompson.ca` | Impact on Fine Art Collector Experience | Modern Redesign Solution Implemented |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Outdated BaseKit / GoSiteBuilder Engine** | Stiff template layout, slow media rendering, difficult mobile navigation | Bespoke vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JS architecture with silky smooth responsiveness |
| **Default `enomcentral` Favicon** | Generic domain registrar icon displays in browser tabs instead of artist branding | Custom luxury SVG monogram (`HT` with radiant gold aura halo) |
| **No French Language (EN Only)** | Loses French-Canadian art patrons (Montreal / Senvest) & European collectors (Switzerland/France) | **Full bilingual toggle (EN / FR)** across all biographies, quotes, catalog items, and forms |
| **Single Lighting Mood** | Cannot visualize artwork under evening museum lighting or daylight conditions | **Dual-Theme Toggle (White Gallery / Dark Museum)** with persistent state |
| **Lack of Spatial Scale Context** | Hard for collectors to gauge how 66" × 90" or 50" × 60" canvases look in real homes | **Interactive Architectural Room Visualizer** (Collector Loft, Penthouse, Gallery Suite) |
| **"2019 Available Catalog" Label** | Creates the impression that recent 2020–2026 inventory is uncataloged or sold | Dynamic curated portfolio with filter tabs, pricing, and live inquiry hooks |
| **No Transparent Commission Estimator** | High-net-worth clients and interior architects hesitate without ballpark budgets | **Art Advisory & Commission Estimator** (canvas size, series, framing, insured freight) |
