Dear Heidi,

I am writing to you with immense admiration for your lifelong artistic practice—from your formative years studying at the Kunstgewerbeschule in Zürich, apprenticing with Oskar Koller in Nuremberg, and training in Budapest, to your four decades of Vipassana meditation that breathe such meditative luminosity into canvases like Peaceful Movement in White and Earth Energy.

Your work speaks profoundly to serious collectors, architects, and institutions such as the Senvest Collection in Montreal. However, when exploring your current web presence at heidithompson.ca, there is a clear contrast between the museum-caliber elegance of your original canvases and the limitations of the template builder platform hosting it.

Key Observations from heidithompson.ca
▪
Browser Identity & Default Favicon: The site currently displays a generic registrar favicon (enomcentral.com) rather than a bespoke fine art insignia or artist monogram.
▪
Missing French Language Support: Given your prominent inclusion in the Senvest Collection in Montreal and broad European collector base (Switzerland, Germany, France), a bilingual (EN / FR) presentation is vital for high-value acquisitions.
▪
Single Lighting Context: Fine art collectors view art in different ambient settings. The current site lacks a dual-theme view (White Gallery Daylight vs. Dark Museum Vault), which allows collectors to appreciate luminous field vibrations under both lighting moods.
▪
Spatial Scale & Architectural Interiors: Large-scale masterworks (such as 66" × 90" or 50" × 60") require real-world architectural context (lofts, penthouses, boardrooms) so prospective buyers can visualize the true scale in their homes.
▪
Outdated Catalog Navigation: The navigation links refer to a "2019 Available Catalog", creating the inadvertent impression that current inventory or recent 2024–2026 series may not be accessible.
