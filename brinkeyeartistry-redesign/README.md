# Brink Eye Artistry — Luxury Redesign

A modern, high-converting redesign for **Brink Eye Artistry** (`https://brinkeyeartistry.com/`), Kelowna's certified SoftTap microblading and semi-permanent makeup studio founded by **Sarah DeFehr**.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🌓 Dynamic Light & Dark Mode:**
  - Warm silk ivory with champagne gold accents (Light mode)
  - Deep obsidian noir bronze with radiant gold accents (Dark mode)
  - Automatic system preference detection + manual toggle with local storage persistence.
  - Dynamically updates the brand logo between standard and reversed versions.

- **✨ Interactive Before & After Slider:**
  - Interactive split drag-slider showing client brow transformations from natural baseline to fully healed hair-stroke embroidery.

- **💰 Interactive Treatment & Cost Estimator:**
  - Real-time calculator letting visitors pick services (Initial Microblading, Powder Fill, Pigment Boosts, Upper Eyeliner, Full Lip Blush, Lash Lift & Tint).
  - Calculates estimated appointment duration, bundle savings, and friend promotion discounts.
  - One-click transfer from calculator into the appointment request form.

- **📐 The Sarah DeFehr Clinical Advantage:**
  - Highlights Sarah's 15+ years of clinical dental hygiene experience, bringing medical-grade sterilization, delicate dexterity, and gentle topical numbing protocols that calm nervous clients.
  - Features 100% vegan, synthetic, heavy-metal-free pigments with only 5–10% micro-fading (no blue/orange color shifts).

- **📋 4-Step Brow Journey & Pre/Post Care Roadmaps:**
  - Clear breakdown of consultation, 5% lidocaine numbing, stroke application, and 6-week perfecting boost.
  - Day 1 to 21 healing roadmap with After Ink care instructions and contraindication checklist.

- **📱 Fully Responsive & Mobile-First:**
  - Mobile slide-out drawer, sticky navigation header, responsive pricing cards, and interactive Google Map preview for 255 Benchview Road, Kelowna, BC.

- **✉️ Promotional Outreach Package:**
  - `EMAIL_OFFER.md`: Professional outreach copy tailored for Sarah DeFehr.
  - `promotional-email.html`: Responsive HTML email template for email marketing.
