Client Protocol

Pre & Post Treatment Guidelines

Following these guidelines guarantees maximum pigment retention, crisp hair strokes, and seamless recovery.

Before Your Appointment: DO NOT

  • Blood thinners 24–48 hours prior: Avoid alcohol, aspirin, ibuprofen, vitamin A, and vitamin E to minimize bleeding and scabbing.
  • Caffeine on day of appointment: Minimize caffeine intake to reduce sensitivity and skin flushing.
  • Direct sunburn: Deeply tanned skin has excess melanin and unpredictable pigment retention.
  • Botox, chemical peels or laser: Avoid these treatments for at least 2 weeks prior to your brow appointment.
  • Heavy exercise on procedure day: Elevated blood pressure increases bleeding during tattooing.

Day 1 to 14 Recovery Roadmap

Night of Procedure:

Gently wipe brows with a warm, damp face cloth and apply a thin layer of provided After Ink cream.

Week 1 (Days 2–7):

Apply After Ink cream 3–4 times daily with clean hands or Q-tip. Brows will appear darker; slight redness is normal.

First 10 Days: Avoid:

No direct sun exposure, no swimming/hot tubs/saunas, no heavy sweating, and face away from direct shower spray.

Day 21 & Beyond:

Pigment settles fully into the dermis, revealing that soft natural hair-like finish ready for your week 6 touch-up!

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question before booking? Here are the most common inquiries from our Kelowna clients.

Discomfort is typically minimal! We apply a 5% Lidocaine topical numbing ointment for 20–30 minutes prior to procedure, plus a secondary comfort liquid throughout the application. The brow area has very few nerve endings, and many clients describe the sensation as a light scratching or tweezing.
Microbladed brows typically last between 1 to 2 years, depending on your skin type (dry, normal, or oily), lifestyle, UV sun exposure, and skincare regimen. An annual or bi-annual pigment boost will keep your arches crisp and vibrant.
Skin takes a full 6 weeks for pigment to settle into the upper dermis. During the initial healing, 5–10% micro-fading occurs as the skin regenerates. The perfecting session is what locks in stroke density, evens out any spots that shed, and ensures your results last up to 2 years.
We exclusively use premium-grade, synthetic, 100% vegan pigments with zero heavy metals or cheap iron oxides. Unlike old-fashioned tattoo inks that turn red, orange, or navy blue as they age, our inks fade gently and true-to-tone by only 5–10%.
