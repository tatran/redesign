Our process

Built from the ground up.

  1. 01

    Assess the site

    We check the slope, drainage and existing base, then explain your options in a written quote.

  2. 02

    Build a stable base

    We remove weak material where needed, grade for drainage and compact fresh aggregate.

  3. 03

    Pave and inspect

    We lay hot-mix asphalt at a thickness suited to your traffic, compact it and walk the finished job with you.

Paving FAQs

How much will my driveway cost?

Price depends on the size, site access and the amount of base or drainage work. After a free site visit, we’ll give you a written quote with the options.

Can you pave over my existing driveway?

Sometimes. We assess the condition of the existing asphalt and the stability of the base before recommending an overlay or a rebuild.

Do you fix drainage before paving?

Yes. We check where water flows and can correct the grade or install drains before laying asphalt.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We provide a written one-year warranty.
